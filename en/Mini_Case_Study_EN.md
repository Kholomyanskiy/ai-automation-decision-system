# MINI CASE STUDY
## AI Chatbot for Customer Support

---

## COMPANY:

SaaS company, $2M annual revenue, 15 employees

---

## PROBLEM:

• 200 email requests per week from customers
• 2 people in support spend 30 hours/week
• Response time: 24+ hours
• Cost: $4,000/month (support salaries)

---

## SOLUTION:

• AI chatbot (ChatGPT API) for L1 support
• Integration: Intercom + Zapier + ChatGPT
• Knowledge base for AI training
• Implementation time: 2 weeks
• Setup cost: $2,000 (consultant)
• Monthly cost: $400 (API + tools)

---

## RESULTS:

**WAS → BECAME:**

• Emails: 200/week → 80/week (-60%)
• Response time: 24 hours → 2 hours (-92%)
• Cost: $4,000/month → $1,600/month (-60%)
• Satisfaction: 3.2/5 → 4.5/5 (+41%)

---

## KEY TAKEAWAY:

60% of simple questions can be automated, freeing team for complex cases.

---

## ROI CALCULATION:

**Investments:** $2,000 (setup) + $400/month × 12 = $6,800

**Savings:** $2,400/month × 12 = $28,800/year

**ROI:** ($28,800 - $6,800) / $6,800 = **323%**

**Payback period:** 0.8 months **(24 days)**
