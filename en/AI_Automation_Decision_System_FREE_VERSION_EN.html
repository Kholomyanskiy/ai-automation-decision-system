CHAPTER 1.2: HOW THE METHODOLOGY WORKS

The Big Picture (5 Steps)

The entire audit process fits into 5 simple steps:

STEP 1: Process Inventory
 ↓
STEP 2: Analysis with AI
 ↓
STEP 3: ROI Calculation
 ↓
STEP 4: Prioritization
 ↓
STEP 5: Action Plan

Let's go through each one.

STEP 1: Process Inventory (30 minutes)

What you do:

Write down all repeating processes in your business.

How:

1. Open the e-commerce checklist (it's in Part 2)

2. Answer questions about your store

3. Fill in a simple table:

Process Who does it How much time How often
Order processing Manager O. 3 hours/day Daily
Social media replies Myself 1 hour/day Daily
And so on...
Result:

A list of 10-15 processes with time costs.

STEP 2: Analysis with AI (45 minutes)

What you do:

Take a ready-made prompt from this guide, insert your data, send to Claude/ChatGPT.

Example prompt: 

Hello! I own an online store [your niche].

Here are my main processes:
1. [process 1] — [time]
2. [process 2] — [time]
...

Help me:
1. Find processes that can be automated
2. Suggest specific tools
3. Estimate implementation complexity

AI will tell you:
  • Which processes to automate
  • Which tools to use (with names)
  • Approximate cost and complexity
Result:

A list of 3-7 processes to automate with recommendations.

STEP 3: ROI Calculation (30 minutes)

What you do:

Take the ROI calculator (Excel file, included), enter data.

What you enter:
  • Current costs (salary × time on process)
  • Automation cost (one-time + monthly)
  • Expected time reduction (usually 50-80%)
Calculator will show:
  • Monthly savings
  • Payback period
  • Annual ROI
Example: 

Process: Order processing
Current costs: $800/month
Automation cost: $500 + $50/month
Time savings: 70%

Result:
→ Savings: $560/month
→ Payback: 0.9 months (27 days)
→ Annual ROI: $6,220

Result:

You know exactly which processes pay off to automate and which don't.

STEP 4: Prioritization (15 minutes)

What you do:

Arrange processes by "Value × Complexity" matrix.

 
       HIGH VALUE
            ↑
 [3]        | [1]
 Medium ROI | Best ROI
 Hard       | Easy
 ───────────┼───────────→ LOW COMPLEXITY
            |      
 [4]        | [2] 
 Poor ROI   | Quick wins
 Hard       | Easy
            ↓
      LOW VALUE

Quadrant [1]: DO FIRST
  • High ROI + Easy to implement
  • Example: Social media auto-responder
Quadrant [2]: QUICK WINS
  • Low ROI, but very simple
  • Example: Email templates
Quadrant [3]: DO SECOND
  • High ROI, but complex
  • Example: CRM integration with warehouse
Quadrant [4]: DON'T DO
  • Low ROI + Complex
  • Example: Custom development for minor tasks
Result:

You understand in what order to automate.

Example plan: Month 1: Quick wins
  • Week 1-2: Chatbot for typical questions

  • Result: -20 hours of work/month
Month 2-3: Main projects
  • Order processing integration
  • Auto-reports
  • Result: -40 hours/month, savings $2k/month
Month 4-6: Optimization
  • Refinements
  • Team training
  • Scaling
Result:

A concrete plan of what to do and when.

What If I Get Stuck?

Three common problems: Problem 1: "AI gives general advice, not specific tools" Solution:

Clarify the prompt. Add:

Name specific SaaS solutions with prices.
Which exact integrations to set up?

Problem 2: "Don't know how to calculate hourly cost" Solution:

Simple formula:

Monthly employee salary ÷ 160 hours = hourly cost

Example:
$1,600/month ÷ 160 hours = $10/hour

If you do it yourself:

Desired salary ÷ 160 hours = your hourly cost

Problem 3: "I have too many processes, don't know where to start" Solution:

Choose top-3 most expensive (by time).

Only analyze those.

80/20 rule: 20% of processes consume 80% of time.

Find that 20%.

How to Use Prompts

A prompt is an instruction for AI. Like a Google query, but more detailed.

In this guide you'll find ready-made prompts.

Here's how to use them:

Step 1: Find the prompt in the guide

They'll be in boxes like this:

[PROMPT]
Hello! I own an online store...

Step 2: Copy the prompt text
  • Select all text
  • Ctrl+C (or Cmd+C on Mac)
Step 3: Paste into Claude/ChatGPT
  • Open chat with AI
  • Paste prompt (Ctrl+V)
Step 4: Replace [FILL IN] with your data

In prompts there will be places like:

My niche: [FILL IN]
Number of orders: [FILL IN]

Replace with your data:

My niche: sports nutrition
Number of orders: 50/day

Step 5: Send
  • Press Enter
  • Wait for response (10-30 seconds)
Step 6: Clarify if needed

AI might give a general answer.

Then add:

Give specific tool names with prices.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: I don't know exactly how much time processes take. What to do?

A: Estimate roughly. Or time it for one day.

Example:

  • Today time how long manager processes orders
  • Multiply by 22 working days
  • Enter in calculator

±20% accuracy is not critical.

Q: I have no employees, I do everything myself. How to calculate hourly cost?

A: Ask yourself: "How much do I want to earn?"

Example:

  • Want $3,000/month
  • 3000 ÷ 160 hours = $18.75/hour
  • That's your hourly cost

Or:

  • How much does it cost to hire a freelancer for this task?
  • Use that number
Q: ChatGPT gives different answers each time. Is this normal?

A: Yes, normal. AI generates text probabilistically.

What to do:

  • If answer too general → clarify prompt
  • If completely wrong → rephrase question
  • Can ask same question 2-3 times and choose best answer
Q: Do I need to pay for Claude Pro / ChatGPT Plus?

A: No, for one audit free version is enough.

Paid is needed if:

  • You'll do many audits (for different projects)
  • Want faster and more detailed answers

For the first time — free is sufficient.

Q: I'm not good with Excel. Will the calculator be complicated?

A: No. You just enter numbers in yellow cells.

Everything else calculates automatically.

If really not comfortable with Excel:

  • Open in Google Sheets (simpler)
  • Or calculate manually by formula:

Savings = (Time × Hourly cost × Reduction%) - Monthly costs

1. Process analysis → 1 request
