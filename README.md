# AI Automation Decision System

A practical methodology for deciding which business processes to automate — and how.

Free version includes Part 1: Quick Start (30–45 min read).

**Available languages:** Russian · Polish · English

---

## What this is

Most businesses approach automation backwards: they pick a tool first, then look for problems to solve with it. This methodology inverts that — it starts with your processes, calculates real ROI, and only then recommends tooling.

The system answers three questions:
1. Which processes in my business are worth automating?
2. What ROI can I expect, and how do I calculate it before spending money?
3. What are the common failure patterns to avoid?
